About OBMC

Learn more about our history, mission, and Montessori philosophy.

Our Story

Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center (OBMC) was founded to bring authentic Montessori education to the Philippines. We focus on helping students grow intellectually, socially, and emotionally so they become responsible and independent citizens.

Our Mission

To provide a prepared learning environment that nurtures self-discipline, curiosity, and a lifelong love for learning in every student.

Our Campuses

We serve families across multiple campuses including Greenhills, Las Piñas, Fairview, Angeles, and Sta. Ana.
